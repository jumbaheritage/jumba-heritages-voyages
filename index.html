Your Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the group safari packages?

Airport pickup/drop-off (JKIA), luxury hotel in Nairobi, premium 4x4 safari vehicle, luxury lodges/camps in the parks, all meals on safari, game drives, park entry fees, bottled water, and professional guide. Minimum 10 people per group.

How does the invoice payment work?

Click "Request Invoice" on any package, fill out the form. We will email you a Payoneer payment link within 24 hours. You can pay with credit card or bank transfer - no Payoneer account needed.

What vehicles do you use for group safaris?

Custom-modified Toyota Land Cruisers with pop-up roofs, WiFi, and refreshments. Each vehicle can accommodate 6-8 guests for optimal game viewing.

Can we customize a group safari?

Yes! Contact us directly and we will create a custom itinerary for your group based on your preferences, budget, and desired destinations.

What is the minimum group size?

Our group packages require a minimum of 10 people. For smaller groups, please contact us for custom pricing and arrangements.
