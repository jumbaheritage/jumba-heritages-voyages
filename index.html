Royal Migration Safari — Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Nairobi

Pickup from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA). Transfer to luxury hotel in Nairobi for overnight rest.

Accommodation: Hemingways Nairobi or Villa Rosa Kempinski (5-star)

Meals: Breakfast included

Day 2

Maasai Mara Safari

Early morning departure (5:00 AM) to Maasai Mara. Afternoon game drive spotting lions, elephants, and more.

Accommodation: Angama Mara or Mahali Mzuri (luxury tented camp)

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner included

Day 3

Departure

Sunrise game drive (6:00 AM - 9:00 AM) to see predators. Return to Nairobi and airport drop-off.

Meals: Breakfast included

End of safari. Departure from JKIA.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before embarking on your Kenyan adventure.

What is included in the Royal Migration Safari?

Airport pickup/drop-off, 1 night luxury hotel in Nairobi (breakfast), premium 4x4 safari vehicle, 1 night luxury tented camp in Maasai Mara, all meals in Mara, two game drives, park entry fees, bottled water, and professional guide. Minimum 10 people.

How does the invoice payment work?

Click "Request Invoice" on any package, fill out the form. We will email you a Payoneer payment link within 24 hours. You can pay with credit card or bank transfer — no Payoneer account needed.

What vehicles do you use?

Custom-modified Toyota Land Cruisers with pop-up roofs, WiFi, and refreshments. All Kenyan registered (K-plates). Each vehicle is equipped with first aid kits, charging ports, and binoculars.

Do you offer transportation only?

Yes, we provide premium transportation services to all Kenyan destinations including airport transfers, inter-city travel, and custom routing. Contact us for bespoke transportation quotes.

What is the minimum group size for the Royal Migration Safari?

The Royal Migration Safari requires a minimum of 10 people at $1,500 per person. For smaller groups, please contact us for custom pricing — we can arrange private departures with adjusted rates.

What is your cancellation policy?

90% refund for cancellations 60+ days before departure.
50% refund for cancellations 30-60 days before departure.
No refund for cancellations less than 30 days before departure.
We strongly recommend travel insurance to cover unforeseen circumstances.
