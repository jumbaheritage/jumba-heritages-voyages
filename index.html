Samburu National Reserve | 3 Days / 2 Nights | $2,400 per person (Min 10 people)

Day 3

Samburu → Nairobi

Morning game drive (6:00 AM - 9:00 AM). Return to Nairobi with lunch en route. Airport drop-off for evening flights.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch

Group Safari Package

Tsavo Group Safari — Detailed Itinerary

Tsavo East & West | 4 Days / 3 Nights | $2,500 per person (Min 10 people)

Day 1

Nairobi → Tsavo East

Morning pickup from JKIA. Drive to Tsavo East (approx 4 hours). Afternoon game drive to see red elephants, lions, and giraffes.

Accommodation: Ashnil Aruba Lodge or Voi Wildlife Lodge

Meals: Lunch, Dinner

Day 2

Full Day Tsavo East

Morning and afternoon game drives. Visit Lugard Falls, Aruba Dam, and the vast open plains teeming with wildlife.

Accommodation: Ashnil Aruba Lodge or Voi Wildlife Lodge

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3

Tsavo West Exploration

Drive to Tsavo West. Visit Mzima Springs (hippos and crocodiles), Shetani Lava Flows, and the Chaimu Crater.

Accommodation: Kilaguni Serena or Severin Safari Camp

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 4

Tsavo → Nairobi

Morning game drive. Return to Nairobi with lunch en route. Airport drop-off for evening flights.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch

Tsavo East & West park fees

Morning game drive (wild dogs, lions, leopards). Afternoon walking safari with Samburu guides — learn tracking and bush survival skills.

Conservancy Exploration

Visit a local Samburu village for cultural immersion. Afternoon game drive to spot rare species like Grevy's zebra.

Accommodation: Ol Malo Lodge or Loisaba Tented Camp

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 4

Laikipia → Nairobi

Morning game drive. Flight back to Wilson Airport. Transfer to JKIA for departure.

Meals: Breakfast

Naivasha & Hell's Gate — Detailed Itinerary

Great Rift Valley | 3 Days / 2 Nights | $3,000 per person (Min 10 people)

Day 1

Nairobi → Lake Naivasha

Morning pickup from JKIA. Drive to Lake Naivasha (approx 2 hours). Afternoon boat safari to see hippos, fish eagles, and over 400 bird species.

Full day at Hell's Gate — cycling and walking safaris through dramatic gorges. Spot zebras, giraffes, and antelopes up close. Visit geothermal spa.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3

Crescent Island & Departure

Morning guided walk on Crescent Island — walk among giraffes, zebras, and wildebeest. Return to Nairobi. Airport drop-off.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the group safari packages?

All group packages include airport pickup/drop-off, luxury accommodation, premium 4x4 safari vehicles, all meals as specified, game drives/activities, park entry fees, and professional driver-guides. Minimum 10 people per group.

How does the invoice payment work?

Click "Request Group Quote" on any package, fill out the form. We will email you a Payoneer payment link within 24 hours. You can pay with credit card or bank transfer.

What is the minimum group size?

All group safari packages require a minimum of 10 people. For smaller groups, please contact us for custom pricing.

What is your cancellation policy?

90% refund for cancellations 60+ days before departure. 50% refund for cancellations 30-60 days before departure. No refund for cancellations less than 30 days before departure.
